Flat front-on 1536x1024 screenshot, no device frame.

Design: stylish, clear, welcoming public directory. Neutral light stone gray background #E5E6E5, charcoal #252727 header and typography, warm yellow #F1D36A active accents, restrained coral and green in category icons. Main background visibly gray, never pure white. Compact crisp Chinese sans-serif typography, 6px card radii, thin gray borders, minimal shadows, original elegant visual identity. Avoid beige/brown/orange-dominated palette. Navigation itself is the product.

Layout: full-width slim charcoal header, ToolDawn logo prominent at left with simple small yellow sunrise mark. Header links '工具导航', '热门工具', '最近更新'; right '中文' globe, quiet '登录' link. No sidebar dashboard, no account avatar, no personal files, no recent user history. Centered max-width 1280px directory body.

At top of content, a modest 30px heading '工具导航', on the same row a spacious central search field '搜索工具名称或用途' with search icon and compact yellow '搜索' command. No marketing hero or big slogan. Under this a full-width flat category navigation bar with small line icons and exact labels '全部工具', '图片处理', '文字写作', '日常生活', '实用工具'. Selected category has a short yellow underline. A fine divider separates navigation from directory.
